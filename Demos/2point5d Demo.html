How 2.5D Turning Works

Behind the scenes, Stickman is walking on a flat 2D grid map (see the radar on the right!). Pressing A or D rotates Stickman's viewing cone so you can turn 90° down side hallways.

Try turning around corners & walking 360° around the floating stars!
Use W/S to walk and A/D to turn Stickman!
